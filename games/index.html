Cooper The Hawk

My first game on the web! Who said being a bird was easy? Travel though the city and experience the difficulties of being a hawk!

Download (Windows)

Download Here

Instructions: Unzip and run "CooperTheHawk.exe"

Note: The game is just a demo. Levels 5 and 6 aren't meant to be actual levels, but rather the testing ground. Also, I am aware of the unfair difficulty of some sections and I will keep it in mind for future works.

Gameplay

Controls

  • LEFT, RIGHT: Moving
  • UP: Jump
  • UP (on air): Gliding
  • SPACE (on air): Grab
  • SPACE (while grabbing): Shoot
  • Q: Pause
